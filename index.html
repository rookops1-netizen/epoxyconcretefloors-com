Frequently Asked Questions About Epoxy Flooring

How much does epoxy flooring cost?

Epoxy flooring costs typically range from $3 to $12 per square foot depending on the type of system, preparation required, and geographic location. Standard garage epoxy with flake system averages $4-$7 per square foot. Metallic epoxy floors run $8-$12 per square foot. We provide free, detailed estimates for every project. Our estimates include surface preparation, materials, labor, and any necessary repairs. We also offer volume discounts for larger commercial and industrial projects.

How long does epoxy flooring last?

Professional epoxy flooring lasts 15-20 years for residential applications and 10-15 years for commercial/industrial use with proper maintenance. The key to longevity is proper surface preparation and using 100% solids epoxy systems. Our installations include comprehensive warranties for your peace of mind. With regular cleaning and occasional re-coating of the top clear coat, your epoxy floor can look great for decades. We provide detailed maintenance instructions with every installation to help you maximize the life of your floor.

How long does epoxy floor installation take?

A standard garage epoxy floor installation typically takes 2-3 days. Day 1 is surface preparation (grinding, crack repair, cleaning). Day 2 is primer and epoxy coating application. Day 3 is the top coat and curing. The floor is typically walkable after 24 hours and ready for vehicles after 72 hours. Larger commercial projects take longer depending on square footage. We work efficiently to minimize downtime and will provide a detailed timeline before any work begins so you can plan accordingly.

Can epoxy be applied over existing concrete?

Yes, epoxy can be applied over existing concrete as long as the concrete is structurally sound, properly cleaned, and free of sealers, coatings, or contaminants. We use diamond grinding to prepare the surface for optimal adhesion. Existing cracks can be repaired, and the floor can be leveled if needed. We assess every concrete surface before installation. Our pre-installation assessment includes moisture testing, concrete hardness testing, and a thorough inspection for cracks, spalling, and other issues that need to be addressed before coating.
