Types of Commercial Epoxy Systems

We offer several commercial-grade epoxy systems. Self-leveling epoxy provides a smooth, seamless, high-gloss finish ideal for showrooms and retail spaces. Quartz-filled epoxy systems add texture and slip resistance for wet areas. Heavy-duty epoxy mortar systems provide maximum durability for industrial applications. ESD (electrostatic discharge) epoxy systems protect sensitive electronic equipment. UV-resistant polyurethane topcoats maintain appearance in sun-exposed areas. We recommend the best system based on your specific needs and traffic levels.

The Commercial Epoxy Installation Process

Commercial epoxy installation follows a rigorous process designed to minimize disruption to your business. We start with a thorough site assessment to understand your needs and schedule requirements. We prepare the concrete surface by diamond grinding or shot blasting to create proper adhesion. We repair any cracks, spalls, or damage. We apply the epoxy system according to manufacturer specifications, typically including a primer, base coat, and topcoat. We can add slip-resistant aggregates, color flakes, or custom logos. We allow proper curing time before the floor is ready for use. We work around your business hours, often scheduling work after hours or on weekends to minimize downtime. The result is a beautiful, durable floor that enhances your business environment.

Return on Investment for Commercial Epoxy Floors

Commercial epoxy floors deliver excellent return on investment. The long service life — typically 10-20 years with proper maintenance — means you won't need to replace your flooring frequently. The low maintenance requirements reduce ongoing cleaning costs. The seamless, easy-to-clean surface improves hygiene and reduces the risk of contamination. The attractive appearance enhances your brand image and creates a positive impression on customers. The reflective surface improves lighting efficiency, reducing energy costs. And the durability protects your concrete substrate from damage, preventing costly repairs. When you consider all these benefits, commercial epoxy flooring is one of the best investments you can make in your facility.

Frequently Asked Questions About Commercial Epoxy Floors

How much do commercial epoxy floors cost?

Commercial epoxy flooring costs vary based on system type, square footage, and site conditions. Most commercial installations range from $3-8 per square foot. We provide free estimates with detailed pricing.

How long does commercial epoxy installation take?
