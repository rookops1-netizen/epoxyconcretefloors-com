Why Epoxy for Basement Floors?

Basements present unique challenges for flooring. Moisture, humidity, and potential flooding make many flooring materials unsuitable. Epoxy is the ideal basement floor solution because it creates a waterproof barrier that prevents moisture from migrating through the concrete. It's impervious to stains and easy to clean — perfect for basements that may see spills, muddy feet, or pet accidents. Epoxy floors are also durable enough to withstand heavy furniture, exercise equipment, and high traffic. And they reflect light, making basements feel brighter and more spacious.

Our Basement Epoxy Process

Our basement epoxy installation follows a thorough process. We start with moisture testing to ensure the concrete is suitable for coating. We repair any cracks or damage. We diamond grind the surface to create proper adhesion. We apply a moisture-blocking primer, followed by the epoxy base coat. We can add decorative flakes, metallic pigments, or solid colors. We finish with a durable topcoat. The result is a beautiful, waterproof floor that transforms your basement into usable living space.

Basement Moisture and Epoxy Flooring

Moisture is the biggest challenge for basement flooring, and epoxy is uniquely suited to address it. Epoxy creates a waterproof barrier that prevents moisture from migrating through the concrete slab. However, proper moisture testing is essential before installation. We perform calcium chloride tests or relative humidity tests to measure the moisture vapor emission rate of your concrete. If moisture levels are too high, we may recommend a moisture mitigation system before coating. In some cases, a vapor barrier may need to be installed. In others, a specialized moisture-tolerant epoxy system may be used. Our thorough testing and proper system selection ensure your basement epoxy floor performs well for years, even in challenging moisture conditions.

Basement Epoxy Floor Cost vs. Other Flooring Options

When comparing basement flooring options, epoxy offers excellent value. Carpet costs $2-5 per square foot installed but is susceptible to moisture damage and stains. Luxury vinyl plank costs $3-7 per square foot and offers good moisture resistance but can be damaged by heavy furniture. Ceramic tile costs $5-15 per square foot and is durable but cold and hard underfoot. Epoxy flooring costs $3-6 per square foot installed, is completely waterproof, extremely durable, and easy to maintain. When you consider the combination of durability, moisture resistance, and low maintenance, epoxy offers the best value for basement flooring. Plus, epoxy can be installed over existing concrete without the need for underlayment or subfloor preparation.
