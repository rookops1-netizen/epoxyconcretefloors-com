What Is Concrete Polishing?

Concrete polishing is a multi-step process that mechanically grinds and refinishes concrete to create a smooth, glossy surface. Using progressively finer diamond grinding tools, we remove surface imperfections, expose the aggregate, and create a dense, hard surface that reflects light like polished stone. The process can achieve various levels of gloss, from a matte satin finish to a high-gloss mirror finish. Polished concrete is then sealed with a penetrating sealer that protects the surface and enhances the appearance.

The Concrete Polishing Process: From Grinding to Gloss

Concrete polishing is a multi-step process that transforms ordinary concrete into a beautiful, high-gloss surface. The process begins with coarse diamond grinding (typically 30-50 grit) to remove surface imperfections, coatings, and contaminants. Subsequent passes use progressively finer diamond tools (100, 200, 400, 800, 1500, and 3000 grit) to refine the surface and increase the gloss level. After grinding, a densifier is applied to harden the concrete and reduce porosity. The final step is applying a penetrating sealer that protects the surface and enhances the appearance. The entire process typically takes 3-7 days depending on the desired level of gloss and the condition of the concrete. The result is a durable, beautiful floor that can last the lifetime of the building.

Polished Concrete Finish Options

Polished concrete can achieve various levels of gloss, from a subtle matte finish to a high-gloss mirror finish. A matte or satin finish (400-800 grit) provides a natural, understated look that's ideal for industrial and commercial applications where slip resistance is important. A semi-gloss finish (1500 grit) offers a good balance of appearance and practicality for retail and office spaces. A high-gloss finish (3000 grit) creates a mirror-like surface that's stunning for showrooms, lobbies, and high-end retail. We can also expose the aggregate to varying degrees — from a light salt-and-pepper look to full aggregate exposure that reveals the beauty of the stone in your concrete. Each finish level has different cost and performance characteristics, and we'll help you choose the right one for your space.
