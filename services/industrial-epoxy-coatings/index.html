Industrial Epoxy Coating Systems

Industrial environments require specialized epoxy coating systems designed for extreme conditions. Epoxy mortar systems combine epoxy resin with aggregate to create a thick, extremely durable coating that can withstand heavy impact, thermal shock, and chemical exposure. These systems are ideal for manufacturing areas, loading docks, and heavy equipment zones. 100% solids epoxy systems contain no solvents and provide maximum film thickness in a single application — they're ideal for areas that need to be back in service quickly. Chemical-resistant epoxy systems are formulated to withstand specific chemicals including acids, solvents, and caustics. Anti-static epoxy systems dissipate static electricity, protecting sensitive electronic equipment and reducing the risk of sparks in flammable environments. Each system is engineered for specific conditions and performance requirements.

Surface Preparation for Industrial Coatings

Proper surface preparation is critical for industrial epoxy coatings. Industrial concrete floors are often contaminated with oils, grease, and chemicals that must be removed before coating. We use industrial-grade shot blasting or diamond grinding to create the proper surface profile for adhesion. We repair cracks, spalls, and damaged areas. We test for moisture vapor emissions and address any issues. We apply primers and base coats according to manufacturer specifications. The quality of the surface preparation directly affects the performance and longevity of the coating system. Our experienced crews follow strict protocols to ensure proper preparation every time.

Safety and Compliance for Industrial Floors

Industrial floors must meet various safety and compliance requirements. We can formulate epoxy coatings with slip-resistant additives to meet OSHA safety standards. We can create color-coded zones for traffic management, safety areas, and storage zones. We can apply anti-static coatings for environments that require ESD protection. We can install cove bases and floor-to-wall transitions for sanitary environments. We can provide documentation of coating specifications and installation for compliance purposes. Our industrial epoxy systems are designed to help you meet regulatory requirements while providing the durability and performance your facility needs.
